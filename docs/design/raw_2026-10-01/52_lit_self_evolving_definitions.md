**Short answer:** the 2025–2026 literature has no single agreed meaning of "self". The big surveys use "self-evolving agent" broadly, and their scope doesn't exclude an external LLM acting as the optimizer. One 2026 overview explicitly allows teacher models. Inside harness evolution, though, the "same-model" camp is growing. It treats a stronger proposer as a confound ("knowledge transfer", "distillation") and calls that setup external or outer-loop optimization, not self-improvement. With Opus 5.5 as proposer, the defensible label is "harness evolution with an external (stronger) proposer", the same category as ADAS and Meta-Harness. It is not "self-improving" in the strict sense, and not RSI.

## 2. Explicit statements about who plays which role

**Stronger external proposer, labelled as external search:**
- **ADAS** (Hu, Lu, Clune; ICLR 2025; 2408.08435): "the meta agent uses GPT-4, while the discovered agents… are evaluated using GPT-3.5." "Higher-order ADAS", where the meta agent improves itself, is left as future work [verified].
- **Meta-Harness** (Lee, …, Khattab, Finn 2026; 2603.28052): the proposer is "Claude Code with Opus-4.6". Targets include GPT-OSS-120B and Haiku 4.5. The paper calls itself an "outer-loop system that searches over harness code", not self-improving [verified]. This is the closest analogue to an Opus-5.5-proposer setup.

**Same model in every role, with the reason stated:**
- **Self-Harness** (Zhang et al. 2026; 2606.09498; benchmarks include **AppWorld**): "the same fixed model… invoked in a proposer role". "Unlike recent approaches that use stronger external agents to improve the harnesses of weaker ones… Self-Harness seeks to internalize this improvement loop." The paper's abstract says improvement happens "without relying on human engineers or stronger external agents." Models tested: MiniMax M2.5, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, GLM-5 [verified].
- **ACE** (2510.04618; also on AppWorld): "we use the same LLM for the Generator, Reflector, and Curator (non-thinking mode of DeepSeek-V3.1), preventing knowledge transfer from a stronger Reflector or Curator to a weaker Generator" [verified]. This is the clearest precedent for treating a stronger diagnoser as a confound.
- **TT-SI vs TT-D** (Acikgoz et al. 2025; 2510.07841): self-improvement means "the same model generates…". When "a stronger model generates" the data, they call it **Test-Time Distillation** [verified].

**Self-referential setups, where the agent edits its own code with frozen weights:**
- **STOP** (Zelikman et al.; 2310.02304): uses GPT-4. "Since the language models themselves are not altered, this is not full recursive self-improvement" [verified].
- **Gödel Agent** (2410.04444): agents modify their own logic "without relying on predefined routines or fixed optimization algorithms" [verified].
- **SICA** (Robeyns et al.; 2504.15228): "eliminates the distinction between meta-agent and target agent". Uses Claude 3.5 Sonnet plus an o3-mini reasoning sub-agent; model weights are not modified [verified].
- **DGM** (Zhang, …, Clune; 2505.22954): Claude 3.5 Sonnet (New) does the self-modification, with "frozen pretrained FMs". The paper's key assumption is that better coding means better ability to self-modify. Future work: "rewriting its own training script to update the FM itself" [verified].

**Zero-data self-evolution (weights):** Agent0 (2511.16043) uses two agents "initialized from the same base LLM" and no external data [verified].

## 3. Is an Opus-5.5 proposer "OPD"?

Strictly, no. OPD samples the student's trajectories and has "a high-performing teacher… grade *each token*" with reverse KL, then updates the student's **weights** (Thinking Machines, Lu et al., 2025-10-27 [verified]). Yin et al. 2026 (2609.30652) define OPD with an "external teacher" and on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) as the version that "eliminates the need for the external teacher" [verified].

Your setup has the same structure, though: a stronger model reads the executor's own rollouts and writes corrective signal. The difference is that the signal lands in the harness, not the weights. The literature's names for this are "knowledge transfer from a stronger Reflector" (ACE) and "test-time distillation" (Acikgoz et al.). Calling it "teacher-guided" or "external-optimizer" harness evolution is fair. (This mapping is my own synthesis, not a source's claim.)

Under the old setup, if gpt-4o really is weaker than Qwen3.8-27B (your premise; I didn't check it), the capability-transfer concern is mostly absent. It comes back with Opus 5.5.

## 4. Where there is consensus

1. **Frozen weights plus an evolving scaffold is not "full" RSI.** The authors of STOP, DGM and SICA say so themselves. The 2026 surveys treat it as a separate branch (scaffolding vs FM; deployment-time vs training-time).
2. **No human editing each change** is the minimum for "self". Environment or unit-test feedback is generally accepted (Gao: "environment feedback"; Yang: "fully automated").
3. **The broad term "self-evolving agent"** in the Gao and Fang surveys counts systems whose optimizer is a separate LLM. They grade autonomy as a range rather than excluding such systems.

## 5. Where it is contested

1. **Must the improver be the improved model?** Permissive camp: Yang 2026, Gao, Fang, and ADAS/Meta-Harness practice. Strict camp: Self-Harness, SICA, ACE (as a fairness control), TT-SI vs TT-D, Tao 2024's framing, and Ren et al.'s word "endogenous".
2. **Must the improver itself improve?** STOP, Gödel Agent, DGM, Chen 2026 ("modifies… the machinery of improvement itself") and Liu & van der Schaar ("intrinsic") all say this is what makes something recursive. Fixed outer loops are bounded refinement at most.
3. **Must there be zero external data?** Agent0-style zero-data work makes this the defining feature. Harness-evolution papers don't require it.
4. **Fixed vs evolving evaluator.** For Chen 2026 this is where bounded refinement ends and RSI begins.

## 6. What this means for the paper

- **Opus 5.5 as proposer or diagnoser:** call it "external-proposer harness optimization", like Meta-Harness or ADAS. Avoid "self-improving" and especially "RSI". Reviewers who know Self-Harness and ACE will raise the distillation confound.
- **To claim "self-evolving":** add a same-model arm, with Qwen3.8-27B as both proposer and diagnoser. Keep gpt-4o (weaker) and Opus 5.5 (stronger) as ablations on proposer strength. Report the gap between them as knowledge transfer. That turns the objection into a result.
- **To claim recursion:** let the proposer edit its own prompts or procedures, as in STOP, DGM or SICA. Disclose whether the evaluator (AppWorld unit tests) stays fixed. By Chen 2026's definitions, a fixed evaluator means bounded refinement.
